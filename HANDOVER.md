# MPOWERED handover

MPOWERED helps people record their weekly health experiences, review their health information and prepare for appointments. This page brings together the user and developer guides, requirements, and ownership/licensing information for the client and future maintainers.

The six supporting PDFs are stored in [docs/handover](docs/handover/), downloaded or exported from Confluence on 5 November 2026. Local PDF links work from a checkout of this repository; the source links open the corresponding Confluence pages.

## Start here

- [Open the web application](https://maxliu3616.github.io/musculoskeletal-mpowered/).
- [Read the user manual](docs/handover/MPOWERED-User-Manual.pdf).
- [Browse the local handover files](docs/handover/).
- [Open the source repository](https://github.com/MaxLiu3616/musculoskeletal-mpowered).
- [Open the Confluence project space](https://musuculoskeletalhealth.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/MA).
